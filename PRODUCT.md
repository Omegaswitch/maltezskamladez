# Product

## Platform

Web.

## Stack

Static site: HTML and CSS generated by a Python 3 standard-library build script from files in this repository. No framework and no CMS. Three small serverless functions (`api/`) serve the staff publishing page. The code base is the Grand Priory's site (maltezskyrad.cz). Production deployment requires explicit authorisation.

## Users

- Young people with disabilities aged 15 to 40 and their families, who look for trips, camps and company ("guests").
- Prospective volunteers (18+), including nursing students.
- Donors and supporters.
- Members and friends of the Grand Priory.

Many visitors have a disability themselves: the site must work with a keyboard, a screen reader, large text and on a phone.

## Product purpose

The website of Česká maltézská mládež (Czech Maltese Youth), a work of the Czech Grand Priory of the Sovereign Military Hospitaller Order of St. John of Jerusalem of Rhodes and of Malta. It says who the group is, what it does, how to join as a guest or a volunteer, how to ask for a grant and how to support it, and it carries the news from its trips, camps and concerts.

## Operating context

- The content comes from WordPress at cmm.maltezskyrad.cz (12 pages, 42 posts 2018–2025, about 400 media files), imported on 15 November 2026 (`docs/content-inventory.md`).
- Volunteers write the news a few times a year, usually from a phone or a laptop after an event, with many photographs.
- Consent forms and the grant application are run by the Grand Priory on www.maltezskyrad.cz; this site links to them.
- Governing references: *Sovereign Order of Malta Worldwide Communications Policy and Guide* (Rome, February 2025), *Guidelines for correct use of the two emblems and of the flags of the Order of Malta* (v3.3, 1 September 2023), and the Grand Magistry *Entities Theme* (March 2025).

## Capabilities and constraints

- Languages: Czech (source), English, French, Italian, German. Translations are labelled "translation – awaiting review" until a native speaker has checked them. The news is in Czech only.
- Names: the full name of the Order at first mention and in the banner; then "Suverénní maltézský řád" or "Maltézský řád". Never "Malta" alone. No acronyms, neither for the Order nor for Česká maltézská mládež.
- Emblems: this is an activity, so the red shield, with the Order's name in the page language. No emblem is created, redrawn, recoloured or animated. The old logo is not compliant and is not used (`docs/brand-compliance.md`).
- Apolitical and neutral. No invented facts, figures, quotations or endorsements: new texts state only what the existing site states.
- The footer links to www.orderofmalta.int; banner, colours and layout follow the Grand Magistry theme.
- Photographs show identifiable people, some of them minors and many in a vulnerable group: consent must be confirmed before launch (`docs/asset-manifest.md`).
- No cookies, no analytics, no third-party scripts except the optional AddCal calendar. Facebook is a plain link.

## Product principles

1. One Order: every page shows the group as part of the Order and leads to orderofmalta.int and to the Grand Priory.
2. People first: guests and volunteers find "how do I join" within one click from the home page.
3. Truth over polish: authentic content is preserved, everything unreviewed is marked, nothing is invented.
4. Easy for volunteers: publishing a post with photographs takes a few minutes and needs no technical knowledge.
5. Safe by default: the staff page is the only door, and it is a narrow one.

## Accessibility and inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: generous type, high contrast, keyboard and screen-reader support, reduced motion respected.
